## Decisions & assumptions
*(Made per the operating rules; items marked 🔔 are flagged as blocking questions in plan.md §9 — confirm before P3/P4 lock.)*

- **One money module** (`lib/money.ts`), integer paise, branded; no float currency math anywhere.
- **CGST/SGST odd paisa → CGST** (`cgst=ceil(total/2)`); direction pinned by a golden test.
- **One normalized store** (`Normalized<T>={byId,allIds}`), immer+persist `hew-erp-v1`, derived state never stored.
- **Dispatch has no `unitId`** — scoped only via parent inward (single `scopedDispatches` choke point).
- **Single Module enum + role presets** in `types/rbac.ts`; `can()` is the only check (guards + actions).
- **Invoice grain = per Bill No, and the Bill No *is* our GST invoice number** ✅ — “Rolex Invoice No” is the customer’s ref only. A **DRAFT invoice auto-attaches on dispatch save** (FR-BI01); the Billing module finalizes it (consignee + issuer + PDF).
- **Stock per `(unit, part)`; grouping `(unit, challanNo, partId)`; consignee at billing** ✅ *(SRS FR-ST02 / FR-RM03a)*.
- **MR + MF both consume stock; neither billed** ✅ *(SRS FR-OUT02/05)* — the 176,921 reconciliation holds.
- **GST% is a per-part master attribute** (12% Rolex / 18% others) ✅ *(UC-03 / BRD)*.
- **FY for numbering from the bill/invoice date** 🔔; seq counter `issuerId:FY`; **undo never decrements seq** (gaps OK, reuse never).
- **taxKind/totals snapshot at issue** (legal immutability — sole exception to “never store derived”); derivation uses **issuer** state.
- **Rejection consumption not reversible in v1** 🔔 (rework deferred); **Rejection-Advice weight basis = finishWt** 🔔.
- **TCS = flat 1% on (value+GST)** 🔔, kept configurable *(scrap billing is added scope beyond the SRS)*.
- **Weights integer mg/grams; rates 2-dp (paise)**; 3-dp rates rejected at entry.
- **Mock auth** (pick seeded user; password ignored) — frontend-only, not real security.
- **Out of scope for v1:** credit notes, partial-quantity billing across two invoices, scrap-weight auto-derivation.
- **Reconcile invariant corrected:** the naive `opening+received=consumed+closing` is always-true and catches nothing; replaced with **I1** (per-inward `Σchildren.totalQty ≤ received`), **I2** (per-part derived stock ≥ 0), **I3** (referential integrity + billed↔invoice), **I5** (optional physical `StockSnapshot` match).
